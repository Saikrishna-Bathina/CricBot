# RAG Evaluation Plan & Benchmark Metrics

## 1. Objectives
Ensure every cricket law answer generated by the assistant is:
1. Grounded strictly in official MCC and ICC documents.
2. Accompanied by verifiable clause and page citations.
3. Accurate in distinguishing general MCC Laws from competition-specific ICC conditions (e.g. Free Hits, Over rate penalties, Timed out durations).
4. Free of invented laws or hallucinated citations.

## 2. Evaluation Metrics

| Metric | Target | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Retrieval Recall@5** | > 95% | Fraction of queries where the relevant clause is in the top 5 chunks |
| **Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR)** | > 0.85 | Position of the ground truth clause in retrieval ranking |
| **Citation Validity** | 100% | Percentage of displayed citations that resolve to real database records |
| **Abstention Quality** | 100% | Correctly abstains on non-cricket or unsupported questions |
| **Grounding Accuracy** | > 95% | Factual alignment between generated answers and retrieved text |

## 3. Evaluation Dataset
The curated evaluation dataset resides at `server/src/services/rag/evaluationDataset.js` and contains:
- Direct law queries (e.g. helmet struck behind wicketkeeper)
- Exact law number queries (e.g. Law 31 Timed Out)
- Non-striker run out queries (Law 41.16)
- Competition-specific conditions (e.g. ICC T20I Free Hit on all no-balls)
- Boundary catch edge cases (Law 19.5.2)
- Over rate penalties & stop clocks (ICC Clause 13.7)
- Unsupported / out-of-domain queries (abstention test)
