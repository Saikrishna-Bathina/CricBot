## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, collegiate-grade adjudication terminal tailored for match referees, elite umpires, and sports governance boards. The aesthetic marries traditional Marylebone Cricket Club (MCC) archival prestige with modern real-time dispute resolution technology. 

- **Personality:** Decisive, forensic, scholarly, and uncompromisingly precise.
- **Target Audience:** International and domestic match officials, third umpires, integrity officers, sports litigators, and institutional analysts requiring instant legal verification.
- **Design Movement:** High-contrast archival editorial crossed with brutalist legal utilitarianism. It features razor-thin ruled borders, dense split-pane dossiers, strict typographic hierarchy, and tactile brass and wax-seal signifiers.
- **Atmospheric Standard:** No generic AI chatbots, neon glows, rounded cartoon cards, or amorphous drop shadows. Interfaces behave like active legal ledgers, binding statute codices, and precision technical instruments.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in historic cricket heritage—the deep, near-black evergreen of match caps and heavy flannel blazers, the warm ivory of archival rulebooks, and the rich vegetable-tanned crimson of a Duke ball.

- **Primary (`#0F241D` - Forest Pine / Deep British Racing Ink):** Anchors statutory headers, structural borders, authoritative ribbons, and high-emphasis textual pronouncements.
- **Secondary (`#9B2226` - Match Ball Crimson):** Dedicated strictly to infractions, penalty runs, dismissals, disciplinary codes, and irreversible determinations.
- **Tertiary (`#C89B3C` - Umpire Brass):** Applied to ratified certifications, law amendments, sovereign match stamps, protocol validation badges, and clause anchors.
- **Neutral (`#1C2421` - Inkstone Neutral):** High-density body text, structured data values, and fine line rules.
- **Surface Foundations:**
  - Archival Canvas: `#FBFBF9` (Parchment base)
  - Surface Substrate: `#F4F3EE` (Muted docket tray, collapsible margins, and statutory footnotes)
  - Pure Ground: `#FFFFFF` (Active split-pane reading sheets and editable adjudication drafts)
- **Rules & Dividers:** High-contrast hairline rules rendered in `#D8D6CC` or semi-transparent deep ink `rgba(15, 36, 29, 0.14)`.

## Typography

The typographic engine uses a tripartite system built for judicial rigor:

1. **Statutory Headlines & Dictums (Newsreader):** Brings editorial dignity and gravitas to Law Titles, Appendices, Case Studies, and Adjudication Pronouncements. Always set in optical sizes with standard ligatures enabled.
2. **Statutory Prose & Legal Text (Inter):** Highly legible, neutral sans-serif engineered for continuous multi-paragraph reading of MCC clauses, playing conditions, and witness statements. Set with generous line-heights to support margin notations.
3. **Forensic Codes & Telemetry (JetBrains Mono):** Monospaced, tabular data handling Law numbers (e.g., `LAW 31.3.2`), ball trajectory coordinates, UTC match timestamps, penalty run ledgers, and official protocol tags. All monospaced labels must be strictly capitalized.

## Layout & Spacing

The workspace uses a dense, split-pane asymmetric grid inspired by legal reference folios:

- **Desktop Structure (>= 1280px):**
  - **Left Rail (Index / Codex Pane):** Fixed 280px width, containing nested law chapters, search filters, and case indexes.
  - **Center Workspace (The Brief / Interaction Canvas):** Fluid 60–65% width containing the active query, match state telemetry, and synthesized legal rationale.
  - **Right Rail (Annotated Statute & Clause Margin):** Fixed 380px collapsible panel displaying literal verbatim MCC sub-clauses, precedence transcripts, and historical rulings.
- **Rhythm & Grid:** Built on an absolute 4px/8px micro-grid. Information density is prioritized over excessive blank padding.
- **Dividers:** Columns are demarcated not by wide air pockets, but by razor-thin vertical rules (`1px solid #D8D6CC`) mimicking ruled court dockets.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects decorative drop shadows, blurred glass layers, or floating Z-index elevations. Depth is communicated strictly through surface layering, structural ink borders, and crisp mechanical insets:

- **Base Layer (Level 0):** Parchment desktop `#FBFBF9`.
- **Secondary Tray (Level 1):** `#F4F3EE` bounded by `1px solid #D8D6CC` for inactive panes, collapsed dossiers, and pinned protocol lists.
- **Forensic Panel (Level 2):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface reserved for active decision-making nodes, current clause viewports, and editable ruling notes. Framed by a double-rule edge (`1px solid #0F241D` inset with `1px solid #E5E3D8`).
- **Critical Alerts & Dismissal Overlays:** A solid `2px` crimson `#9B2226` outer border with a non-blurred 4px offset hard shadow: `4px 4px 0px rgba(15, 36, 29, 0.25)`.
- **Tactile Insets:** Input fields, filter trays, and scrub bars feature an inverted 1px top line (`border-top: 1px solid rgba(15, 36, 29, 0.12)`) simulating debossed parchment.

## Shapes

The design system enforces complete geometric austerity (`roundedness: 0`). 

- Every card, button, tab, badge, input field, and split-pane divider has sharp 90-degree corners (`border-radius: 0px`).
- Evoking cut parchment, physical ledger cards, and precision mechanical instrumentation, this zero-radius framework eliminates playful casualness and affirms legal finality.
- Specialized indicators (e.g., status seals, stamped certifications) employ sharp clipped-corner chamfers (45-degree angle cuts via CSS clip-path) rather than curved radiuses.

## Components

### 1. Buttons & Adjudication Triggers
- **Primary Sovereign Action:** `#0F241D` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `1px solid #0F241D`, zero radius. `Newsreader` font or bold `JetBrains Mono`. Hover transitions to `#16382C` with a `1px` inner brass hairline.
- **Infraction / Uphold Appeal Action:** `#9B2226` background, `#FFFFFF` text, uppercase `JetBrains Mono` label, hard edge.
- **Secondary / Clause Reference:** `#F4F3EE` background, `#0F241D` text, `1px solid #D8D6CC`. Hover: `#EAE8DF`.

### 2. Clause Citations & Law Badges (Chips)
- Structured monospaced tags (e.g., `LAW 38.2.1: RUN OUT`).
- Background `#F4F3EE`, bounded by `1px solid #D8D6CC`, text in `#0F241D` (`label-md`).
- Clickable chips feature an appended brass section symbol (`§`) in `#C89B3C`.

### 3. Split-Pane Dossiers & Adjudication Cards
- Surfaces rendered in `#FFFFFF` with a continuous `1px solid #D8D6CC` border.
- Headers are marked by a deep Forest Pine band (`#0F241D`) or a top hairline border of `2px solid #C89B3C` indicating ratified status.
- Zero drop shadows; content boundaries are reinforced via monospaced metadata bars (`SESSION: 2 | OVER: 48.4 | BALL: 4`).

### 4. Input Fields & Query Terminals
- Flat `#FFFFFF` or `#FBFBF9` surface with `1px solid #0F241D`.
- Caret is rendered as an authoritative square terminal block in `#9B2226`.
- Placeholder text in muted archival ink (`#8A8980`) with monospaced format examples (`e.g., "MCC 33.2 fielder contact with boundary cushion while airborne"`).

### 5. Checkboxes, Radios, & Ball-by-Ball Verification Toggles
- Square indicators (`16x16px`) with `1px solid #0F241D`, no border-radius.
- Active state displays an ink-stamp cross (`✕`) or filled internal square in `#0F241D` or `#9B2226`.

### 6. Domain-Specific Component: The MCC Clause Concordance Card
- Displays verbatim MCC statutory text side-by-side with match incident transcripts.
- Left edge features a vertical brass bar (`3px solid #C89B3C`).
- Margin annotations (glosses) sit flush right in `body-sm`, colored in `#6B6A62` with monospaced cross-references (`vide Appendix D.1`).
