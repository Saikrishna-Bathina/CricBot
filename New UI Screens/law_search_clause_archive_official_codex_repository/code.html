MCC LAW 21.1.1 Mode of delivery
verified MCC 2017 Code (3rd Ed.) • Folio 22

"21.1.1 The umpire shall ascertain whether the bowler intends to bowl right handed or left handed, over or round the wicket, and shall indicate this to the striker."

Failure to notify the striker of a change in mode of delivery constitutes an immediate infraction requiring the bowler's end umpire to call and signal No ball at the instant of delivery.

MCC LAW 21.10 Ball bouncing more than once
history_edu MCC Canonical Statute • Folio 22

"21.10 Ball bouncing more than once: The umpire shall call and signal No ball if a ball which the bowler attempts to bowl pitches more than once before reaching the popping crease."

Precedent Interpretation (Elite Panel Standard):

The trajectory must touch the surface between wickets at least two discrete times prior to the line of the striker's crease. A rolling ball is legally defined as an infinite succession of bounces and falls strictly under this statute.

ICC PLAYING CONDITIONS § 21.19 Free Hit After Any No Ball
T20I & ODI MANDATORY • Oct 2023 Edition

"21.19 Free Hit after any No ball: The delivery following a No ball shall be a free hit for whichever batter is facing. If the delivery for the free hit is not a legitimate delivery, then the next delivery will become a free hit."

Field Restriction Clause:

Field changes are prohibited if the same batter remains on strike, unless the No ball occurred due to a fielding restriction breach.

Permitted Dismissals:

Run Out (Law 38), Hit the Ball Twice (Law 34), Obstructing the Field (Law 37). All other dismissal modes suspended.

ICC Specific Variation Test Match: Non-applicable
MCC LAW 21.19 Penalty for a No ball
gavel MCC Standard Code • Folio 23

"21.19 Penalty for a No ball: A penalty of one run for a No ball shall be awarded to the batting side. This penalty shall be in addition to any other runs scored or penalties awarded."

info The penalty run counts as an Extra and shall not be credited to the striker's individual score.
