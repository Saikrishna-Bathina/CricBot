Definitive Statutory Determination
“The protective equipment penalty supersedes potential dismissal in transitu: under Law 28.3.2, the ball becomes immediately dead the instant contact occurs with the placed helmet. Consequently, no batsman may be given out Run Out pursuant to Law 38.3.1, and 5 penalty runs must be awarded forthwith to the batting side.”

HIERARCHY STATUS CLAUSE 28 PREVAILS
Discretionary Scope gavel

Zero on-field umpire latitude exists to overlook the dead-ball call or allow play to continue. The 5 penalty runs are statutory and mandatory.

Bowler Record Exemption: Penalty runs are conceded by fielding side extras, never debited against bowler analysis (Law 28.3.3).
OFFICIAL MANDATE NON-DISCRETIONARY
Verbatim Statutory Codex • Law 28.3 (Protective Equipment) p. 64
Authentic Recension 2017 Code (3rd Ed. - 2022)

28.3.1 No protective equipment other than helmets, which shall conform to the specifications laid down in Law 1.2, shall be placed upon the field of play.

28.3.2 If the ball while in play strikes a helmet placed upon the ground within the field of play, the ball shall immediately become dead and, unless the striker is out by any Law other than Law 38 (Run Out), the umpire shall:

• 28.3.2.1: award 5 penalty runs to the batting side;

• 28.3.2.2: signal the penalty runs to the scorers;

• 28.3.2.3: award any runs completed by the batters before the ball struck the helmet, together with the run in progress if the batters had already crossed at the moment of impact.
