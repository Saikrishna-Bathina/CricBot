MCC Law 21 — NO BALL (Clause 21.10) & Law 20.4 (Dead Ball)

The umpire shall call and signal No ball immediately upon the second bounce prior to the striker's crease, and award statutory penalties pursuant to the prevailing competition regulations.

Phase 01: Delivery Stride Abrupt Halting Law 41.2 Adjudication

Bowler enters delivery stride, comes to an unnatural halt, but proceeds to cast the ball toward the striker. If this stop was deliberate and designed to distract the striker, it activates Law 41.4 (Deliberate attempt to distract striker). If merely a faulty run-up without deceptive malice, the delivery is judged strictly on trajectory.

rule Precedent Reference: Law 41.4.1 & Law 21.4 (Bowler throwing the ball)
Phase 02: Multi-Bounce Pitch Incursion Violation: Law 21.10

The released ball pitched three discrete times on the pitch surface before reaching the popping crease of the striker. Under Law 21.10, any ball which bounces more than once prior to the popping crease constitutes an automatic No Ball regardless of intent or bounce height.

warning Threshold Exceeded: Allowable bounces = 1. Actual bounces = 3.
Phase 03: Umpire Mechanical Signaling Law 2.1 & Appendix D

The bowler's end umpire must immediately call and signal 'No ball'. If the ball stops rolling entirely before reaching the striker without contact from the bat, the umpire must simultaneously call and signal 'Dead ball' under Law 20.4.2.6.

front_hand Signal Order: Right arm horizontal horizontally for No Ball > crossed wrists if Dead Ball is signaled.

MCC Law 21.10 (Verbatim)

"The umpire shall call and signal No ball if a ball which he/she considers to have been delivered, without having previously touched bat or person of the striker, bounces more than once or rolls along the ground before it reaches the popping crease."

ICC T20I Free Hit Rule

Under Universal MCC Laws, a No Ball confers a 1-run penalty and an extra delivery. Under ICC T20I Playing Conditions Clause 21.19, all No Balls (front foot, height, or pitch bounce) immediately trigger a Free Hit for the next delivery.
